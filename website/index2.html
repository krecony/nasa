      How the sound is made

      A Python script reads every frame of the video and turns the colors back into numbers using the map's own color bar (for example, this shade of yellow = 2 mm of rain per hour). It then measures how much rain and snow there is, where it is, and how heavy, and turns those numbers into music.

      Visualizations: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio / Earth Information Center. Sonification: Earth Jukebox team.
